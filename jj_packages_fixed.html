Who We Are

Packaging Built With
Precision & Care

Founded in 2008 in Lahore, JJ Packages was built on a simple belief great packaging is more than a box, it's your brand's first impression. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing to deliver solutions that protect, promote, and impress on every delivery.

Today we serve over 500 businesses across Pakistan, offering everything from single unit prototypes to millions of cartons per year, all manufactured under strict quality standards.
